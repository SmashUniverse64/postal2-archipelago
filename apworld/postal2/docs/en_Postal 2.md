# Postal 2

The original Monday–Friday campaign for Archipelago.

Errands, first visits to weekday maps, shops, and uniquely chosen world pickups per generation are location checks.

**Goal:** complete all 19 weekday errands, then return to the trailer.

On New Game you spawn at Home on the YAML **Starting Day** (after the intro, or immediately if you skip it) and stay put until the Postal 2 Client is connected to your room and you receive your first errand for that day. Starting Day is Monday by default, or Tuesday–Friday / Random. The seed then shuffles the other four weekdays into a random beatable order (for example Monday → Thursday → Tuesday → Friday → Wednesday). Leftover chores from your starting weekday sit later in that order, so the first new errands you find are the next shuffled day’s. Checks on one day in that order can hold the next day’s errands, so you always have a path to the rest of the week. You can connect the client after the game is already waiting; the freeze lifts in place.

**Time Warp:** you spawn with and always have access to the brand new Time Warp item. Use it to open a day menu (shows **unlocked** errands). Keys **1–5** pick a day, then a destination: **Home** plus unlockable warp points. After you pick a destination, throw the Time Warp onto the ground. When the light shaft appears, pick it up to warp. If you leave it for ten seconds, the light dies and the warp cancels. Keys **6–0** return to Library, Burning Library, Brewery, Napalm Factory, and Parcel Center after you have left those maps once (same throw-and-pick-up). Your starting weekday is open from the start with one random errand given and a small set of hunt maps you can actually enter that day. Other weekdays unlock when you receive an errand for that day, each one opening another wave of scrounge. Maps never hunt before they are physically open, even if Monday is last in the order. Each hunt map is assigned one weekday: AP tokens and the map-visit check only spawn if you enter that map on that day, so Time Warp back if you miss it. The pause map leaves locked waves unlabeled; once a hunt unlocks, its name stays on the map in gold on its day and grey when you cannot collect there right now. Completing all 19 errands starts the Apocalypse and breaks your Time Warp; walk Home to the trailer to win the game.

**Weapon Repair Kit:** junk/filler (grey AP tokens). With **Melee Durability** on (off by default), melee weapons wear down on connecting hits. Activate a Weapon Repair Kit from your inventory to restore the melee you’re holding to full durability. Picking up a second copy of a melee you already own restores 30 durability. The weapon wheel shows a durability bar on those melee icons, the same way ammo is drawn. ATF and fast-food vending machines sell kits for $50. The Taser does not wear.

**Piss Realism:** off by default. The bladder starts at 1 out of 20. **Piss Regen Seconds** (`piss_regen`, default 120, range 30–180) is seconds per pip. Near 18/20 the Dude complains that he has to pee (the Friday clinic lines). At 20/20 he takes 1 damage per second until he goes. Once he starts peeing he cannot zip up until the meter is under 10. Water bottles are $5 at every vending machine and add 5 to the meter; drinking Crackola also adds 5. Water Bottles are also junk in the item pool (grey AP tokens) when this option is on.

**Hint Flare:** a junk item you activate from inventory. It fires a harmless flare rocket straight up from every AP token on the current map. The rockets pass through buildings and cluster-burst in the sky like rocket-launcher explosions, with one boom per flare, so you can see and hear where the tokens are. ATF and fast-food machines sell them for $30.

**Suicide Bomber Trap:** the suicide bomber from the Tuesday confession / Home marching-band attack spawns unarmed a good distance away on walkable ground and charges you. Shoot them before they get close. Their explosion hits like a grenade.

Visit checks fire once per map, on that map’s hunt day.

All of a day's errands show on the map like vanilla. You still need the matching AP item to finish some of them (paycheck at the bank, library book, petition clipboard). The Dude refuses at the counter if you don't have it yet. Library Book, Clipboard, Citation, and Gift are never placed before the weekday that uses them.

The Stats Clipboard lists remaining errands, in-logic check count, and how many glowing pickups are on the current map. The pause map names areas the same way the game does (Trainyard Area, Industrial Area, Church and Cemetery) and stacks related districts (Napalm Factory Exterior over Napalm Factory). Checkmarks: cyan visited, gold AP tokens done, blue Crackola, magenta ATMs, red vending; a legend is in the bottom-right. Finding the last AP token in an area plays one of the Dude’s map-screen errand-complete lines. In the Postal 2 Client, `/tracker` lists unchecked locations currently in logic (`/tracker monday` for one day, `/tracker count` for the total).

World pickups that are Archipelago locations appear as an **AP token** (the Archipelago logo).

Which extra world pickups become tokens is **chosen per seed** from a catalog of real piles (320-unit spacing). **Max Tokens Per Map** (`max_tokens`, default 12, range 6–15) is the most tokens a hunt map can get; each map rolls 6 through that number, then keeps at most as many piles as it actually has. Each chosen pile is its own named check (`Main Street - Shotgun Stereo House Bedroom`), not `Pickup 01`. Former named stashes (shed shovel, dog-pit rocket, Tora crates) are in that same catalog. Other loot stays vanilla, including medkits and ammo sitting next to a token of the same type. Piles past load zones or inside unbreakable boxes are never tokens. A few piles behind weekday gates are only rolled when that map's hunt day can reach them.

ATF and food vending machines (bank plaza, Office Complex, East Mall, east suburbs) send a check when you open the menu. Each machine can sell unique Archipelago parcels (real item names and scouted prices in the menu). **Max Vending Items** (`vending_slots`, default 3, max 5) is the most parcels a machine can stock; each machine rolls 0 through that number per seed. With Melee Durability on, those machines also sell Weapon Repair Kits for $50 (not an Archipelago check). They also sell **Hint Flare** for $30: a junk item that fires a harmless flare rocket from every AP token on the map. With **Piss Realism** on, every vending machine also sells Water Bottles for $5 (not an Archipelago check). Street Crackola machines (buy or smash) are checks too; each side-by-side pair shares one check, and East Mall has two pairs. Kickable ATMs around town are always checks. With **Token Glow** on, a vending machine, Crackola machine, or ATM uses the rainbow light while any check still on it is useful or progression, and pulses grey when every remaining check is filler or a trap. Token Glow off leaves those lights off.

Vanilla still turns on extra day-tagged loot after Monday. The mutator rescans each map load, so those extra pickups can become AP tokens too.

Options: Starting Day (Monday–Friday or Random), Shuffle Weapons, Max Tokens Per Map, Token Glow, Token Heartbeat (ping gets faster and louder as you near a token), Melee Durability (off by default; melee weapons wear on connecting hits; Weapon Repair Kits restore them; duplicate melee pickups restore 30 durability), Piss Realism (off by default; Piss Regen Seconds 30–180 default 120, damage at 20/20, cannot zip up until under 10, water bottles and Crackola add 5, Water Bottles in the junk pool). The Friday clinic errand is always available (you do not need to have pissed). With Shuffle Weapons on you start unarmed; Dusters and Bali are in the melee unlock pool with the other knives and blunt weapons. Krotchy Grenade, Box Launcher, and Cat Launcher are in the gun unlock pool. Spare Ammo grants 30% of a random owned gun’s max ammo, or restores 30 durability on a damaged melee.
